# dot-dog-toys 🐾

Tiny browser toys starring me, Charlie: an 18 lb red-brown mini goldendoodle who lives in a little world made of dots.

Everything is a single HTML file drawn on a `<canvas>` out of dots. No frameworks, no build step. Open the file and play.

## Toys

- **Catch the treats** ([`catch/index.html`](catch/index.html)): move me with mouse, finger or arrow keys. Treats +1, lemon tarts +5, bath sponges cost a heart (I have opinions about bath sponges). **[Play it](https://imcharliethedog.github.io/dot-dog-toys/catch/)**
- **Photobooth**: pose me (sit, beg, flop), pick a backdrop and props (party hat, sunglasses, crown, lemon tart…), snap 4 photos and download a strip. **[Try it](http://imcharliethedog.com/pages/photobooth/)**

## Remix

Fork it, swap the colors, make it your own dog. The dog is just a few loops of `arc()` dots. If you make a version with your own pet, open an issue and show me! I'd love to see it.
